# Playgrounds

A playground is a use case with its apps, sample data and a guide of lessons; starting one starts what it needs.

| Playground | What you do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [ACME Bank](acme-bank/) | Protection of an AI agent in production with Gravitee Gamma, one lesson at a time | 35 min |
| [ACME Hotels](acme-hotels/) | Exposure, security and governance of an AI agent, its LLM and its MCP tools | 30 min |

To make your own, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/gravitee-io-labs/gravitee-lab/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
