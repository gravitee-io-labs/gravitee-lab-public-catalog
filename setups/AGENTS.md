# Setups

The format of a setup is the Lab's: [the format of add-ons, setups and playgrounds](https://github.com/gravitee-io-labs/gravitee-lab/blob/main/docs/units.md), and what the Lab
promises it is [the contract](https://github.com/gravitee-io-labs/gravitee-lab/blob/main/docs/contract.md). This folder holds the setups of the catalog; `catalog.py check`
(the Lab's `lab-ui/catalog.py`) enforces the rules.
